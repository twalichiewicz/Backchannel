How does it work?

Bring the discussion to you

Stop doing the two-tab tango between what you're reading and the discussion about it. Backchannel puts the conversation right beside the page, with a customizable, repositionable toggle to show it when you want it and hide it when you don't.

See where the conversation is focused

Quoted and heavily discussed passages are highlighted automatically, so you can see where the conversation is concentrated as you read. Click a highlight to jump straight to the comments about that passage.

A new front page of the internet

Don't let silos stop you from finding your next great read. Backchannel blends the sources you choose into a single front page, so what's worth reading can come from anywhere you follow.

Privacy

Triglavis LLC · backchnnl.app · Last updated 16 September 2026

What this site collects

Nothing. There are no cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels and no third-party scripts on this page. Cloudflare hosts it and, like any host, receives each request for it.

What the script does

Backchannel has no server, no analytics and no telemetry. Nothing you read, write or save with it is sent to Triglavis.

  • Your settings, queue, favorites, votes, notes, hidden sites and the comments you have seen stay in your userscript manager's storage, on your device.
  • To find discussions, it asks each source you have turned on about the page you are on. Most are told its address; Lobsters and Mastodon are told only its domain. A source you have turned off is asked nothing.
  • Reddit is the only source that can tell who you are. Signed in, its requests arrive as your account.
  • Voting and replying happen in a window on that source's own site, using the session you already have there.
  • An article opened in the reader loads from the site that published it, with no referrer. If the page does not respond inside that frame, the script fetches it once, without cookies, to see whether it can be shown there and, if not, to show its text.
  • Enhanced PDF support uses pdf.js, downloaded from cdn.jsdelivr.net. That request carries nothing about you.

Every host the script can contact, and exactly what each is told, is listed in SECURITY.md.

Your choices

Turn any source off in Settings, or hide Backchannel on any site. Removing the script, or clearing your userscript manager's data, deletes everything it stored.
